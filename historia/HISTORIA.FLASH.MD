# hISTORIA DE FLASH

The Flash is DC Comics' iconic superhero, famous as the "Fastest Man Alive," with multiple heroes holding the mantle, primarily Barry Allen, a forensic scientist gaining powers from a lightning strike and chemicals connecting him to the Speed Force, enabling super-speed, time travel, and phasing, serving the Justice League, 
often mentoring Kid Flash (Wally West), and battling speedsters and other metahumans in Central City
